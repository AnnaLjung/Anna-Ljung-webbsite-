About the artist

A poetic approach to color, space and material.

Anna Ljung is a Swedish artist whose work exists at the intersection of painting, sculpture, textile art, spatial design, and poetic movement.

Her practice brings Scandinavian restraint together with emotional depth. Each work begins with a tactile conversation — colour, cloth, air, and the space surrounding it.
